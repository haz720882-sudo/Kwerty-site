OPEN SOURCE INTELLIGENCE / TOOLKIT 001

Investigate sources.
Verify, don't assume.

A browser-based workspace for checking public domain records, DNS records, and reputable search sources. No account required. No results are secretly uploaded to KWERTY.

Responsible use: Research domains and information you have a legitimate reason to examine. This tool does not identify private people, track live locations, bypass logins, or scrape private accounts. Public records can be incomplete or outdated.

06 / URL safety checks

Open reputable tools to inspect a URL or domain. Do not submit private links or authenticated URLs.

Third-party services have their own privacy and retention policies. Check them before submitting data.

07 / Investigation notes

Draft a small evidence log locally in this browser. Do not enter passwords, private personal details, or sensitive information.

Notes are stored in this browser's local storage, not on a server. Avoid shared/public devices; clearing browser data may remove them.
